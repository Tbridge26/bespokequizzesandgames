FAQ

Common questions.

Do you travel outside London?

Mainly London, but I'll travel further for the right booking. Get in touch and we can discuss travel costs for events outside the M25.

Do you do virtual quizzes?

Yes. If you're running a virtual event across multiple locations, I can host across video call. The format adapts but the bespoke element stays.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know the date. Christmas party season fills up fast. A few weeks' notice is usually fine for most events, but don't leave it too late.

What do I need to provide?

A venue with space for guests to sit at tables. That's it. I bring mic, PA, laptop, slides, and printed sheets. If the venue has its own AV, even better.

How do prizes work?

You sort the prizes — I'll announce and present them. Doesn't need to be extravagant: a bottle of wine per winning team goes a long way.

Can you do bigger groups?

Comfortably up to 100+. I've run quizzes for 60-person corporate events. For very large groups, I'll adjust the format — get in touch and we can talk it through.

What decides the final price?

Group size and how far I'm travelling. Everything else, the bespoke content, the kit, the hosting, is the same at every price point.

What's not included in the price?

The venue and the drinks. You sort the room and the bar, I bring everything else: mic, PA, slides, printed sheets, and prizes get announced on the night.

Is this team-building or just entertainment?

Both. It's built as a genuinely fun night out first. But because every round is written about your team, people bond over the inside jokes without it feeling like a forced exercise.

What happens on the night?

I arrive early, plug into the venue screen, do a sound check, hand out answer sheets, then we're straight into it. Rounds run back to back, prizes at the end. No dead time waiting for tech to work.

Can everyone join in, whatever their needs?

Yes. Paper answer sheets mean nobody needs an app or a phone. If anyone in the group has specific accessibility needs, tell me beforehand and I'll build the round formats around it.

How is this different from a normal pub quiz company?

Most quiz companies run the same deck at every venue. I write a new quiz for every booking: your team's faces in the picture round, your prices in Price is Right, your inside jokes in the general knowledge. Nobody else in the room has done that quiz before.

Can you theme the whole night?

Yes, that's most of the job. Christmas, Pride, a specific decade, an office in-joke: tell me the theme and every round gets built around it.

How soon should I book for Christmas?

Now, ideally. December fills up faster than any other month. Some companies lock in their Christmas quiz in September. If you know the date, get in touch.

Do you take a deposit?

Full payment is required upon booking, paid via PayPal or bank transfer against an invoice.
