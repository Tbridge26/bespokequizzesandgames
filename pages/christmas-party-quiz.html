FAQ

Christmas quiz questions, answered.

How much is a Christmas party quiz in London?

From £350 for a fully bespoke hosted quiz. That covers writing the quiz around your team, hosting on the night, mic, PA, slides and printed answer sheets. Price depends on group size and travel.

How long does a Christmas quiz last?

Usually 1 to 1.5 hours, which fits neatly between dinner and the dancefloor or into an afternoon office party. I'll recommend the right length for your group.

Can you host at our office or venue?

Yes. I host in offices, pub function rooms, restaurants, bars and private hire venues across London. All I need is tables and, ideally, a screen.

How many people can join?

Anything from 20 to 100+ guests. Teams are formed on the night or in advance, so everyone plays, including people who don't usually sit together.

When should we book?

Now, if you can. Late November and December Thursdays and Fridays go first. Send the date and I'll confirm availability and a quote, usually within 24 hours.

Is it inclusive for the whole team?

Yes. Paper answer sheets, no apps, nothing embarrassing. It works for drinkers and non-drinkers, and any accessibility needs can be built into the round formats.
