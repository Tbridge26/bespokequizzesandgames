Occasions

What kind of event?

Birthdays

A quiz about the birthday person.

Picture round full of their photos, a Price is Right featuring things they've actually bought, music they grew up with. Their friends will be howling at the answers. The guest of honour usually goes bright red at least twice.

Weddings

Reception entertainment that actually entertains.

Fills the reception gap perfectly. Couples vs couples, questions about how well the guests know the newlyweds, a music round mixing both families' decades. Works brilliantly as a 1-hour slot between dinner and dancing.

Stag & hen dos

How well do you really know them?

Questions about the stag or hen that only true friends would know. Embarrassing photos, questionable life choices, that story everyone except the subject finds hilarious. High energy, no filter (within reason).

Other occasions

Any reason to get people in a room.

Leaving dos, reunions, house parties where you want something more than just standing around. If there are people, there's a quiz. I'll adapt the format to what works for your group.

How it works

What makes it bespoke.

Once you book, I send a short form. Takes 10 minutes to fill in. That's all I need to build rounds that feel like they were written specifically for your group, because they were.

  • 📸 Photos of the guest of honour (picture round)
  • 🎂 Key dates and milestones
  • 🎵 Music they actually listen to
  • 💰 Things they've bought (Price is Right)
  • 😂 Moments and stories from friends
  • 🎯 General knowledge themed to them
