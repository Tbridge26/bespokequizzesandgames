What's included

What a company booking looks like.

📋

Before the event

I'll ask you a few questions about the team, names, faces, a bit of history. That's the raw material for the bespoke rounds. You don't need to do anything except answer a quick form.

🎤

On the night

I arrive ahead of time, set up mic and PA, load slides, and distribute printed answer sheets. I host the full quiz, keep time, score the rounds, and hand out prizes at the end.

🎯

The rounds

Picture round featuring your colleagues, a bespoke Price is Right, Higher or Lower, a music round (1.5hr+), and themed questions tailored to the occasion, Christmas, Pride, company anniversary, whatever fits.

👥

Group sizes

Comfortable from 20 to 100+ people. For very large groups I'll adjust the format to make sure everyone stays engaged. Let me know numbers when you enquire.
