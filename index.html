FAQ

Quiz night questions, answered.

How much does a quiz host cost in London?

A fully bespoke hosted quiz starts from £350. That includes writing the quiz around your group, live hosting, mic, PA, slides and printed answer sheets. The final price depends on group size and travel.

What is a bespoke quiz?

A quiz written for one group only. Instead of generic trivia, the rounds feature your people: their photos in the picture round, their office in the Price is Right, their in-jokes in the general knowledge. Nobody in the room has played it before.

Is a quiz a good idea for a Christmas party or team social?

Yes. A hosted quiz gets everyone talking, mixes teams who don't usually sit together and works for 20 to 100+ people. It suits drinkers and non-drinkers alike and needs nothing more than tables and a screen.

Where do you host quizzes?

Across London: offices, pubs, bars, restaurants, private hire rooms and boats. I can travel outside London for the right booking.

When should I book a Christmas party quiz?

As early as possible. Late November and December Thursdays and Fridays are the first dates to go. If you know your date, get in touch for a quote.

What do I need to provide?

A venue with tables and, ideally, a screen. I bring everything else: mic, PA, laptop, slides and printed answer sheets. You sort the prizes and I'll present them.
